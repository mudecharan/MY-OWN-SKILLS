# Competitive Intelligence Methods Reference

## Public evidence sources (free/cheap first)
| Signal | Where | What it reveals |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing pages + Wayback Machine history | competitor site | packaging changes, price trajectory |
| Job postings | careers pages, LinkedIn | roadmap 6–12 months out ("hiring ML engineers" ≠ coincidence) |
| App changelogs | App Store / Play Store | shipped feature cadence and focus |
| Release notes / docs | site | capability coverage matrix |
| Review platforms | G2, Capterra, app reviews | buyer language, complaint themes = positioning axes |
| Filings & registries | annual reports, company registers | revenue bands, headcount growth |
| Community sentiment | Reddit, forums, support communities | real pain points pre-sales |

## Sizing discipline
- TAM top-down (industry report %) AND bottom-up (count × price) — when they diverge >2×,
  your segment definition is fuzzy; fix the definition.
- SOM must be defensible from YOUR funnel/capacity, not ambition.

## SWOT honesty rules
- Each item needs a number or citation; "strong brand" becomes "brand search volume 2.3× nearest rival".
- Opportunities are external and evidenced; wishes are not opportunities.
- Threats get likelihood×impact like any risk register.

## Positioning map axes
Derive from review mining frequency ("ease of use" vs "depth"), never from internal opinion.
Mark where white space is genuinely reachable given your capabilities.
