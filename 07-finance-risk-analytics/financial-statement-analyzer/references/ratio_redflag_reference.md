# Ratio & Red-Flag Reference

## DuPont decomposition
ROE = Net margin × Asset turnover × Equity multiplier
The point: WHEN ROE changes, this tells you which lever did it.
Rising ROE from leverage (3rd term) is lower quality than from margin (1st).

## Working-capital clock
Cash conversion cycle = DSO + DIO − DPO
| Component | Formula |
|---|---|
| DSO | receivables / revenue × 365 |
| DIO | inventory / COGS × 365 |
| DPO | payables / COGS × 365 |

Lengthening DSO with flat revenue growth = channel stuffing smell or collection problems.

## Earnings-quality checks
1. OCF/Net income persistently <1 → accrual-heavy earnings; find why (footnotes).
2. Revenue growing while gross margin falls → buying growth (discounts, loose credit).
3. Capitalized development costs rising sharply → expenses pushed off P&L.
4. One-off "adjustments" recurring every year → they're operating costs.

## Benchmarking
Compare vs industry medians and nearest public comps on BOTH margin structure
and capital intensity. A great ratio in a capital-light industry can be terrible in a heavy one.

## Presentation discipline
Always show 3–5 periods. Single-year ratios are trivia, not analysis.
