# Pyramid Narrative Builder — worksheet

## Step 1: Governing thought (one sentence)
"<Recommendation> because <strongest justification>."
If this can't be written, the analysis isn't finished.

## Step 2: Supporting arguments (MECE, max 3)
| # | Argument | Evidence that carries it | Strength (S/M/W) |
|---|---|---|---|

Weak arguments get CUT, not padded. Two strong arguments beat four shaky ones.
